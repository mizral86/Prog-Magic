Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

This is a translation for convenience. If it differs from the Italian version, the Italian version applies.

Menga Deck is a free, ad-free, non-profit app to manage a collection of Magic: The Gathering cards, build decks, simulate games and play with friends. This page explains which data it uses, why, where it is stored and how to delete it. It applies to the website and to the app installed on your phone.

Who handles the data

The data controller is the developer of Menga Deck. For any question or request about your data write to fenrir.rivervan@gmail.com.

Without an account

You can use the app without signing up. Collection, decks, binders, wishlist, life counter and settings are saved only in your device's browser storage and are not sent to the app's servers. The only things sent are the usage statistics, linked to a random device code and not to your name (you can turn them off), and the problem reports you choose to send.

With an account

If you create an account, the app handles this data:

  • Sign-in data: email address and password (stored only in encrypted form). With Google sign-in we also receive your Google profile name and picture.
  • App data: collection, decks, binders, wishlist and settings, saved on your profile so you find them on every device.
  • Public profile (only if you use the Friends section): username, display name and profile picture. They are visible to people who have a friend request or a group in common with you. Someone who already knows your exact email can find you in search, but your email is never shown.
  • Friends and chat: friend requests, your friends list and the messages you write. Messages are seen only by the sender and the recipient.
  • Online status: while the app is open, your friends see that you are online and the time of your last visit.
  • Groups and tournaments: group names, members, recorded games (players, winner, life totals, duration, notes) and tournaments. They are visible to the group's members.
  • Games at the table with a friend: moves travel in real time between the two players and are not saved on the server.
  • Blocked users and reports: who you have blocked and the reports you send. A report contains the reason, the details you write and a copy of the latest messages (at most 30) between you and the reported person, which only the developer reads to check it. The person reported or blocked is not told.

We use this data only to run the features you choose to use (legal basis: performance of the service you request) and, for reports, to protect other users (legitimate interest). It is not sold, not used for advertising or profiling and not shared with third parties, apart from the technical providers listed below.

Usage statistics

To understand how many people use the app and how much, while the app is open and in the foreground a small summary is sent every minute: how long it has been used, in which sections (Search, Collection, Decks…), whether it is installed and the type of device (Android, iPhone/iPad or computer). The device is recognised by a random code saved in the browser storage, which contains no name, email, phone number or IP address. If you are signed in, the summary is also linked to your account, so the developer can see how long you use it. We do not record what you search for, which cards you own or what you write.

Statistics are stored on Supabase like the other data, only the developer sees them and they are not used for advertising or shared with anyone (legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding how to improve the app). You can turn them off at any time in Account by unticking "Help improve the app with usage statistics". If you delete your account, your sessions remain only as anonymous numbers.

Report a problem

From Account → Report a problem you can send the developer a problem or an idea, even without an account. The report contains:

  • the type (problem or idea) and the message you write;
  • the page of the app you were on;
  • if you leave the box ticked, a screenshot of the page. It may show what was on screen, for example friends' names or chat messages: check it before sending and untick it if needed;
  • if you leave the box ticked, some technical details: app version, browser and system (user agent), screen size, language and theme, whether the app is installed and online, whether you are signed in, how many cards and decks you have, how much storage the app uses and the app's latest errors;
  • the random device code (the same as for statistics) and, if you are signed in, the link to your account.

Reports are read only by the developer, are used only to fix problems and improve the app (legitimate interest) and are not shared with anyone.

Where data is stored

Account data is stored on Supabase (database and sign-in), in a database whose security rules let each person read and change only their own data and the data others have chosen to share with them. The app is published through GitHub Pages (GitHub).

External services

  • Card information, prices and images come from Scryfall: your device downloads them directly, without any account data.
  • Fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts.
  • The scanner uses the camera and reads the card name on the device: images are neither saved nor sent. The text recognition files come from the app's website and, after the first use, stay on the device.
  • Google sign-in goes through Google, under its own privacy policy.

These services may receive the device's IP address, as happens with any website. Some providers may process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (for example standard contractual clauses).

Device storage

The app saves data in the browser storage (localStorage and cache) to work offline and to remember your sign-in session, as well as the random code for the usage statistics described above. It does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools from other companies.

How long

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Messages are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Usage statistics are deleted automatically after 13 months.
  • Problem reports are kept until the developer has handled them and are deleted automatically 6 months after being closed; if you delete your account they remain without a link to your profile.
  • Reports about users are kept as long as needed to handle them and in any case no longer than 12 months; if the person who reported deletes their account, the report remains without a link to their profile.
  • Games recorded in a group remain in the group even if a player deletes their account, with the name written at the time of the game.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from the app: Account → Delete account. Sign-in, app data saved on the profile, public profile, friendships and messages are deleted immediately. Groups you created pass to the longest-standing member. You can choose to keep a copy of your collection and decks on the device.

If you can no longer sign in, you can request deletion by writing to fenrir.rivervan@gmail.com from the account's address: the request is handled within 30 days.

Without an account, clearing the site data in your browser (or uninstalling the app) deletes everything.

Your rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing and receive it in a readable format (the app already lets you export collection and decks). You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Minors

You must be at least 14 years old to create an account (in Italy this is the minimum age to consent to online services on your own). Younger people can use the app without an account.

Contact

For questions, requests about your data (access, correction, deletion) or to report a problem write to fenrir.rivervan@gmail.com. For technical problems you can also open an issue on GitHub, without writing personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. For important changes we let you know in the app.
